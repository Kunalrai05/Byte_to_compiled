## 📐 Mathematical & Aerodynamic Foundation

### 1. Aerodynamic Downforce & Drag
The aerodynamic vertical downforce ($L$) and drag ($D$) generated by the 992 GT3 RS body and swan-neck wing:

$$L = \frac{1}{2} \rho v^2 C_L A$$

$$D = \frac{1}{2} \rho v^2 C_D A$$

Where:
* $\rho = 1.225 \text{ kg/m}^3$ (air density at sea level).
* $v = 79.17 \text{ m/s}$ ($285 \text{ km/h}$).
* In **High Downforce Mode** (DRS closed), total aerodynamic downforce reaches **$860 \text{ kg}$** ($8,436 \text{ N}$), more than twice that of its predecessor (991.2 GT3 RS).
* In **DRS Mode** (upper flap tilted to $34^\circ$), the slot gap relieves high pressure over the wing trailing edge, reducing aerodynamic drag by over **$30\%$** to achieve maximum straightaway velocity.

### 2. 60-Year Geometric Morphing
The transition from the classic 1974 930 Turbo to the 2024 992 GT3 RS is computed via Hermite cubic easing:

$$\mathbf{p}_{\text{blend}}(t) = \mathbf{p}_{930} + S\left(\frac{t - t_0}{t_1 - t_0}\right) \cdot (\mathbf{p}_{992} - \mathbf{p}_{930})$$

$$S(u) = u^2 (3 - 2u), \quad u \in [0, 1]$$
